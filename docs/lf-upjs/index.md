# Student Onboarding Guide

**Faculty of Medicine — Pavol Jozef Šafárik University in Košice (LF UPJŠ)**

Official faculty website: <https://www.upjs.sk/lekarska-fakulta/en/>

## Where is the faculty?

Lekárska fakulta UPJŠ v Košiciach
Trieda SNP 1
040 11 Košice, Slovak Republic

📌 [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Trieda%20SNP%201%2C%20040%2011%20Ko%C5%A1ice)

The Faculty of Medicine is located in the hospital area near Univerzitná
nemocnica Louisa Pasteura Košice. Medical students may have classes, labs or
clinical training at several locations, not only in the main faculty building.
The official LF UPJŠ orientation plan lists the main faculty building, UNLP,
DFN, VÚSCH, AGEL Košice-Šaca, Nemocnica sv. Michala and other clinical teaching
sites.

## Study Programmes in English

Main English-language programmes for international medical students:

| Programme | Length | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Medicine | 6 years | Medical degree programme |
| Dental Medicine | 5 years | Dental medicine programme |

!!! warning
    Slovak language is important for clinical communication. Even if your
    programme is taught in English, you may need Slovak during clinical
    training, hospitals and daily life.

## First Week Checklist

- [ ] Save the faculty address: Trieda SNP 1.
- [ ] Open the official orientation map.
- [ ] Check your exact enrolment instructions.
- [ ] Check your English programme Study Department contact.
- [ ] Activate / check your university login.
- [ ] Log in to AiS2.
- [ ] Check whether you need to complete electronic enrolment.
      Link for manual for electronic enrolment:
      <https://www.upjs.sk/app/uploads/sites/9/2022/10/AIS2-StudentZapisnyList.pdf>
- [ ] Check your timetable and room locations.
- [ ] Register at the Medical Library with your ISIC.
- [ ] Learn how to order subsidised meals.
- [ ] Check dormitory status or private housing backup.
- [ ] Enrol / attend Slovak language course if required.
- [ ] Check university email every day during the first weeks.
