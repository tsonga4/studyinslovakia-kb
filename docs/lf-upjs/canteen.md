# Canteen — Jedáleň / Menza

UPJŠ catering services include several kitchens and serving points, including
Tr. SNP 1, which is the most relevant for LF students. UPJŠ also lists catering
locations at Medická 4, Moyzesova 9, Jesenná 5, Popradská and Mánesova.

## Meal ordering system

UPJŠ uses an online meal ordering system connected with the student card / card
number. Meals can usually be ordered online, at terminals, or at selected
canteen kiosks. The standard deadline for ordering is listed as 14:00 one day
before the meal, with cancellation rules and a "burza" option for unused ordered
meals.

Meal ordering page: <https://www.sdaj.upjs.sk/login>

## Student subsidy system — important

UPJŠ lists a student meal subsidy system. According to the official price
document valid from December 2025:

- ordered student meal: price 7.00 €
- subsidy: 2.40 €
- student pays: 4.60 €
- for free-sale meals, one subsidy applies from 4.00 €
- meals from 8.01 € may receive a higher subsidy according to the listed rules.

!!! tip "Student tip"
    If you want the subsidised price, do not assume every snack or random item
    counts. Check the meal type, order rules and payment system.
