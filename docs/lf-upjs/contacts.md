## International Students Support

For most official study matters, use the English programme Study Department
contacts listed above.

For international mobility / Erasmus / foreign stay information, use the official
UPJŠ and LF UPJŠ pages.

!!! tip "Student tip"
    When writing an email, include:

    - full name,
    - programme,
    - year of study,
    - student number if available,
    - short explanation,
    - attachment if relevant.
