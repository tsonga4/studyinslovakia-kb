# Housing

Where students live in Slovakia, and how to find a place.

## Dormitories

Most universities offer dormitories (*internát*) at low cost. Rooms are usually
shared. Apply through your university as early as possible — places are limited.

## Private rentals

Renting a flat (*byt*) gives more privacy and costs more. Expect to pay a deposit
(often one month's rent) plus utilities.

!!! tip "Watch for scams"
    Never pay a deposit before seeing the place (in person or via a trusted
    contact). If a deal looks too cheap, be cautious.
