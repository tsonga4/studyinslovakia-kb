# Banking

Opening a local account makes rent, bills and daily payments much easier.

## What you usually need

- Passport / ID card
- Proof of study (student confirmation)
- Proof of address in Slovakia

## Tips

!!! tip "Card payments everywhere"
    Slovakia is very card-friendly — contactless works almost everywhere,
    including public transport in many cities.
