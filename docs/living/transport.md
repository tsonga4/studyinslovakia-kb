# Transport

Getting around your city and across Slovakia.

## City transport

Buses, trams and trolleybuses run in most cities. Students can buy discounted
passes — bring your student card.

## Trains & intercity buses

Students often travel **free or heavily discounted** on Slovak trains after
registering for the discount. Intercity buses connect all major cities.

!!! tip "Register for the student discount"
    Sign up for the rail discount as soon as you have your student status —
    it can make train travel free.
