# Health & insurance

Health insurance is **mandatory** for all students in Slovakia.

## EU / EEA citizens

Bring your **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)**. It covers necessary care
during your stay.

## Non-EU citizens

You typically need Slovak health insurance (public or commercial) — often
required for your residence permit too.

## Emergencies

!!! danger "Emergency number: 112"
    Call **112** anywhere in Slovakia for police, ambulance or fire.
    Also: Ambulance **155**, Police **158**, Fire **150**.
