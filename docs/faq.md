# Frequently asked questions

??? question "Do I need a visa to study in Slovakia?"
    It depends on your citizenship. EU/EEA students do not; most non-EU students
    need a residence permit for study. See [Visas & residence](before-arrival/visa.md).

??? question "How do I find accommodation?"
    Apply for a university dormitory early, or look for a private rental. See
    [Housing](living/housing.md).

??? question "Is health insurance required?"
    Yes — it is mandatory for all students. See [Health & insurance](living/health.md).

??? question "How do I get personalised help?"
    Register in the [student portal](https://portal.studyinslovakia.info) to get
    an assigned assistant and information specific to your university.
