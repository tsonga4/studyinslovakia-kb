# Visas & residence

Whether you need a visa depends on your citizenship. Find your situation below.

## EU / EEA citizens

You **do not need a visa** to study in Slovakia. You can enter with a valid ID
card or passport. If you stay longer than 90 days, you register your residence
(see [Registration steps](../after-arrival/registration.md)).

## Non-EU citizens

Most non-EU students need a **temporary residence permit for the purpose of
study**. Start early — the process can take several weeks.

!!! warning "Apply early"
    Book your appointment at the Slovak embassy in your home country as soon as
    you receive your admission letter. Slots fill up before the semester starts.

### Documents you will typically need

- [ ] Valid passport
- [ ] Admission / enrolment letter from your university
- [ ] Proof of accommodation
- [ ] Proof of financial means
- [ ] Proof of health insurance
- [ ] Criminal record extract
