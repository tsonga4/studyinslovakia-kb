# University life

Settling into your studies and campus.

## Enrolment & student card

Complete enrolment at your faculty to receive your **student card** (ISIC),
which unlocks transport discounts, canteens, libraries and more.

## Getting involved

- Student organisations and buddy programmes
- Language courses (Slovak for foreigners)
- Sports and campus events

!!! note "This is a starter page"
    Tailor this to each university and faculty. You can also target content by
    university in the [student portal](https://portal.studyinslovakia.info).
