# Residence permit

For **non-EU students**, collecting and completing your residence permit is one
of the first things to handle after arrival.

## Typical steps

1. Report to the Foreign Police within the required time after arrival.
2. Submit any remaining documents (accommodation, insurance, medical report).
3. Collect your residence card once issued.

!!! warning "Deadlines matter"
    Missing a Foreign Police deadline can cause serious problems with your stay.
    Check the exact timeframe for your permit type.
