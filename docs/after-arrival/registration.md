# Registration steps

A checklist for your first two weeks in Slovakia.

- [ ] Complete university enrolment and get your student card
- [ ] Register your residence (EU citizens: after 90 days)
- [ ] Non-EU: complete Foreign Police steps → [Residence permit](residence-permit.md)
- [ ] Open a bank account → [Banking](../living/banking.md)
- [ ] Sort out health insurance → [Health & insurance](../living/health.md)
- [ ] Get a local SIM card and public transport pass

!!! note "This is a starter page"
    Reorder and expand these steps to match your onboarding flow.
